# Ideas register – from mathematical model to program

**Format:** mathematical topic → what can be calculated or described? → software example → model limitation.

  Topic                                                             Computable problem                                                                              Example                                                                     Limitation
  ----------------------------------------------------------------  ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------  --------------------------------------------------------------------------  ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Number types and variables (School M1-L1)                         Valid value ranges and unknowns                                                                 Input validation, cost formula                                              Real numbers ≠ limited computer types
  Percentages (School M1-L2)                                        Relative fractions of a total                                                                   Progress indicator, success rate                                            Do not confuse percentage points with relative percentages
  Direct proportionality (School M1-L2)                             Cost/time with a constant factor                                                                File processing                                                             Bottlenecks in parallel tasks
  Rules of arithmetic and rearrangement (School M1-L3)              Simplify formula expressions                                                                    Parsers, computational optimisation                                         Floating-point arithmetic and non-commutative matrices
  Coordinates and functions (School M1-L4)                          Positions, value pairs and unique assignments                                                   Sensor readings as a time series, tracking, throughput model                Axis scale, units, discrete data, fluctuating throughput
  Geometry and measurement (School M1-L5)                           Area and volume requirements                                                                    Screen area, textures, 3D construction space                                Idealised shapes and measurement uncertainty
  Statistics (School M1-L6)                                         Frequencies, mean, median, range                                                                Latency analysis of program and AI inference times                          Small sample, outliers, unrepresentative selection
  Powers and roots (School M1-L7)                                   Large and small quantities, squares and roots                                                   Measurements, number of combinations, vector lengths                        Real domains, finite number representation
  Linear functions (School M1-L8)                                   Constant change plus initial value                                                              Data processing rate, tariff models, sensor calibration                     Non-constant rates, discrete events, extrapolation
  Systems of linear equations (School M1-L9)                        Satisfy several conditions simultaneously                                                       Resource allocation, parameter determination, intersection                  Conflicting or dependent conditions, measurement noise
  Pythagoras and distance (School M1-L10)                           Diagonal and Euclidean distance from orthogonal components                                      Screen geometry, 2D/3D simulation, robotics                                 Only right-angled models and straight-line distances; check units and overflow
  Similarity and scale (School M1-L11)                              Convert corresponding lengths proportionally                                                    CAD scaling, maps, simple camera models                                     Perspective, measurement error, lack of parallelism, area factor $k^2$
  Combinatorics and probability (School M1-L12)                     Count possible configurations and calculate event probabilities in a model                      Test case combinations, simulation, risk assessment                         Do not assume equal probabilities; consider dependencies and samples
  Quadratic functions (School M1-L13)                               Curvature, roots and extrema                                                                    Simple optimisation, ballistic sketches, formula evaluation                 Real models may be non-quadratic; numerical instability
  Irrational numbers and rounding (School M1-L14)                   Assess accuracy and allowable deviation                                                         Floating-point operations, measurements, simulation, numerical tolerances   More decimal places do not eliminate measurement errors
  Circles, arcs and solids (School M1-L15)                          Circumference, angular fractions, surface areas and volumes                                     Vector graphics, CAD, 3D geometry, circular robot trajectories              Consider idealised shapes, angular units, measurement units and rounding
  Sine, cosine and tangent (School M1-L16)                          Calculate length components from direction angle and length                                     2D positioning, robot arm geometry, sensor direction                        Requires a right angle, the correct reference side and angle mode
  Growth and decay models (School M1-L17)                           Change by a fixed percentage per step                                                           Capacity estimation, storage inventory, user numbers, compound interest     Constant rate and unlimited growth are often unrealistic
  Functions and inverses (School M1-L18)                            Input-output rules, shifts and inverse calculations                                             Parsers, calibration, graphical transformations                             Check the domain; an inverse function requires uniqueness
  Power, exponential and logarithmic functions (School M1-L19)      Compare different growth patterns and inverse calculations                                      Area and scaling models, search steps, exponentially growing stocks         Consider the base, domain and growth assumptions
  Unit circle and oscillations (School M1-L20)                      Translate angles into coordinates and time into periodic values                                 2D rotation, periodic sensor data, audio signals                            Distinguish degrees and radians, limited sampling and noise
  3D coordinates and vectors (School M1-L21)                        Relate spatial points, displacements and distances                                              Robot grippers, 3D graphics, camera movement, introduction to embeddings    Know the coordinate system, units and types (point/vector)
  Conditional probability and model criticism (School M1-L22)       Diagnose events given additional information                                                    Checker alarms, AI classification, false alarm rates                        Base rate and confusion of $P(A\mid B)$ with $P(B\mid A)$
  Platonic solids and polygon meshes (School M1-L23)                Check symmetry and relationships between vertices, edges and faces                              3D meshes, test geometry, icosahedral refinement                            Convex polyhedra; real meshes may have holes or topological errors
  Function families in calculus (School M2-L1)                      Distinguish mechanisms and types of behaviour                                                   Cost models, capacity models, signal systems                                Curve shape alone does not establish the real model
  Limits and continuity (School M2-L2)                              Describe behaviour near inputs and long-term limits                                             Convergence of iterative algorithms, stability, control variables           The limit is not the function value; identify jumps and gaps in the domain
  Derivative and instantaneous change (School M2-L3)                Local rate of change as the limit of average rates of change                                    Velocity model, sensitivity of AI model parameters, sensor changes          No ordinary derivative without differentiability; measurements are discrete and noisy
  Differentiation and chain rules (School M2-L4)                    Calculate changes in sums, products, quotients and nested functions                             Automatic differentiation, backpropagation, engineering optimisation        Consider zero denominators, corners and domains of the component functions
  Analysis of curves (School M2-L5)                                 Where does a curve rise or fall, and where are maxima, minima and inflection points?            Throughput and sensor characteristic curves, engineering models             Stationary points are not automatically extrema; check domain and boundary
  Optimisation (School M2-L6)                                       Maximise or minimise an objective subject to admissible constraints                             Cost, area and capacity planning, starting point for AI optimisation        A local optimum need not be global; discrete decisions and boundary values matter
  Antiderivative and indefinite integral (School M2-L7)             Reconstruct possible stock functions from a rate of change                                      Displacement from velocity, stock from rate, continuous models              Initially missing constant of integration and initial value; an indefinite integral is not a numerical area
  Definite integral (School M2-L8)                                  Accumulate a signed change over an interval                                                     Traffic flow, data rate, energy consumption, sensor data                    The net total is not automatically a positive geometric area
  Fundamental theorem and stock model (School M2-L9)                Translate between rate of change and total stock                                                Robot motion, queues, dynamic models                                        Initial stock is necessary; discrete measurements require modelling assumptions
  Parametric family of functions and substitution (School M2-L10)   Investigate parameter effects and simplify nested integrals                                     Parameter setting, optimisation, symbolic computer algebra                  Keep parameters constant and transform integration limits when substituting
  Areas, improper integrals, solids of revolution (School M2-L10)   Areas between curves, infinite ranges, 3D solids                                                CAD, engineering geometry, signal and probability models                    Check signs, convergence, axis of rotation and radius
  Vectors and dot product (School M2-L11)                           Determine spatial displacement, vector length and angle from components                         Camera direction, lighting direction, embedding cosine similarity           Zero vector has no angle; consider units and reference frame
  Lines and planes (School M2-L12)                                  Generate points on a line or plane using freely chosen parameters                               CAD, travel directions, camera rays, 3D construction                        Direction vectors must be non-zero or independent as appropriate; restrict finite objects separately
  Relative positions and distances (School M2-L13)                  Check intersections and determine shortest distance to geometric objects                        Collision, ray tracing, obstacle distance and measurement systems           Skew lines, numerical tolerances; do not transfer results uncritically to line segments
  Linear systems as matrices (School M2-L13)                        Arrange coefficients and unknown parameters of a system                                         Simulation, robotics, numerical equation solvers                            Not every system has a unique or stable solution
  Cross product, determinants and rotation (School M2-L14)          Obtain a normal from two directions, check orientation/area scaling and rotate vectors          Ray tracing, CAD, cameras, robot axes                                       det=1 alone does not ensure a rotation; check orthogonality and coordinate conventions
  Conditional probabilities and binomial model (School M2-L15)      Describe dependent events and numbers of successes over multiple trials                         Error models, simulation, quality assurance, AI test series                 Check independence and constant success probability
  Expected value and dispersion (School M2-L16)                     Explain the likely magnitude and range of variation of a distribution                           Latency measurement, benchmarks, risk models                                Mean is not a guaranteed individual value; measurements sensitive to outliers
  Normal distribution and hypothesis testing (School M2-L16)        Standardise values and examine departures from the null model                                   Measurement data comparison, A/B tests, quality control                     p-value is not the probability of $H_0$; check test assumptions and selection
  Upper-secondary mathematics overview (School M2-L17)              Identify a suitable mathematical approach to a problem                                          Robot state: combine motion, geometry and sensor uncertainty                Do not adopt a formula blindly without its validity assumptions
  Set logic and quantifiers (M1-L1)                                 Express unambiguous relationships between elements and propositions                             Types, formal specifications, database conditions, agent rules              Consider quantifier order, empty set and modelling assumptions
  Proof techniques and induction (M1-L1)                            Provide structured justification for universally valid statements                               Algorithm correctness, recursive programs and loop invariants               Many tests are not a general proof

  Differentiation and integrability (M1-L3)                         Establish local change and continuous accumulation mathematically                               Optimisation, energy models, numerical simulation                           Consider corners, jumps, approximation and model limitations

  Vector space and basis (M1-L5)                                    Represent data as independent directions                                                        Parameter space, polynomial representation, embeddings                      Coordinates depend on the basis and chosen scalar field
