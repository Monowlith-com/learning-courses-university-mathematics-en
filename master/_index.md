# Course overview – University · Mathematics {#kurseinstieg}

## Who is this course for? {#S1}

This course is intended for independent learners with upper secondary mathematics who want to deepen mathematical concepts, proofs and models systematically. Start with M1; M11 expands calculus, linear algebra and probability at a higher level. M5–M9 develop algebra, complex analysis, numerical analysis, topology and stochastics; M10 addresses seminar and research methods. Advanced lessons require the stated prerequisites. Core and elective labels are guidance rather than a uniform degree regulation.
