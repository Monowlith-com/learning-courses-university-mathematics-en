# University – Mathematics – Study Path {#studienweg}

The following classification provides an **educational learning path**; it is not a substitute for an official university module handbook or examination regulations. Content, compulsory status, semesters of study and prerequisites vary between universities. **Master’s-oriented** does not mean that a subject is compulsory in every Master’s programme.
